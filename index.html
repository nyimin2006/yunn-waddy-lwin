Why I Wanted to Draw This for You

That Day

That day when I saw you in your Myanmar dress, you honestly looked so pretty in my eyes... almost like a painting that somehow came to life. Some moments are too nice to just let pass by, so I tried to keep a little bit of that moment by drawing you this bouquet and gifting this website to you.

Only for Someone Special

Apart from my mom, you're actually the only second other girl who ever made me want to draw a bouquet of red roses like this.. I don't really do this kinda thing for just anyone. Guess you just have a way of making me want to do something special after seeing you in that dress that day.

Even from Far Away

Since Australia is pretty far away, let these roses go with you in my place... and maybe whenever you look at them, I hope you'll remember the guy who drew them thinking of you.

A Little Poem

So let these roses travel where I cannot go,
And carry a little warmth with them as you go.

These roses may never truly bloom,
But I hope they bring a little beauty to your room.

For some people, I leave a little art behind,
And you're one of those I had in mind.
